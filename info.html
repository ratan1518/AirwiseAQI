Airwise AQI - Understanding Air Quality Index

How We Calculate AQI and Its Impact on Your Health

Step-by-Step AQI Calculation

We calculate AQI based on the concentration of key pollutants. Here's how it works:

Pollutant Concentration (µg/m³) AQI Value
PM2.5 22 µg/m³ 37
PM10 63 µg/m³ 63
SO2 3 µg/m³ 4
CO 257 µg/m³ 13
Ozone 6 µg/m³ 6
NO2 8 µg/m³ 10

The final AQI is based on the highest sub-index, which is 63 for PM10.

Health Effects of Pollutants

Each pollutant has unique health risks at certain concentrations:

Pollutant Effect on Health
PM2.5 Can penetrate the lungs and cause respiratory diseases.
PM10 Can cause eye, nose, and throat irritation; worsen asthma.
SO2 Short-term exposure can cause throat irritation and coughing.
CO High levels can impair oxygen delivery to organs, causing headaches, dizziness, and even death.
Ozone Can cause chest pain, throat irritation, and worsen bronchitis or asthma.
NO2 Can irritate airways in the human respiratory system and cause breathing problems.
Safety Limits

Understanding safe levels of pollutants:

Pollutant Safe Level (µg/m³)
PM2.5 25
PM10 50
SO2 75
CO 1000
Ozone 180
NO2 40
