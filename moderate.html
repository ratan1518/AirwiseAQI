Understanding Moderate Air Quality

What Does "Moderate" Air Quality Mean?

"Moderate" air quality includes AQI ranges where air pollution poses little or no risk to health:

AQI Range Category Health Impacts
51-100 Moderate Air quality is acceptable; however, there may be a risk for some people, particularly those who are unusually sensitive to air pollution.
Health Tips for Moderate Air Quality

Even with Moderate air quality, maintaining healthy habits is beneficial:

  • Enjoy outdoor activities safely.
  • Continue regular exercise and maintain a healthy diet.
  • Stay informed about air quality changes and adjust activities as needed.

Advice

  • Outdoor Activities: Sensitive individuals should limit prolonged outdoor exertion, especially during peak pollution times.
  • Indoor Air Quality: Consider using air purifiers if you are sensitive or live in an area with frequent moderate air quality.
  • Health Monitoring: Monitor your health for any signs of discomfort and take breaks if needed. Ensure proper hydration and avoid strenuous activities outdoors.
