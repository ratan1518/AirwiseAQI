Understanding Hazardous Air Quality

What Does "Hazardous" Air Quality Mean?

"Hazardous" air quality includes AQI ranges where air pollution poses a serious risk to health for everyone. This is the highest level of pollution:

AQI Range Category Health Impacts
301+ Hazardous Health warnings of emergency conditions. The entire population is more likely to be affected.
Health Tips for Hazardous Air Quality and Advice

During hazardous air quality conditions, follow these crucial tips to safeguard your health:

  • Remain indoors at all times and avoid all outdoor activities.
  • Use a high-quality air purifier with a HEPA filter.
  • Close windows and doors to prevent outdoor air from entering your home.
  • Keep hydrated and maintain good indoor air quality.
