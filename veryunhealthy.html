Understanding Very Unhealthy Air Quality

What Does " Very Unhealthy" Air Quality Mean?

" Very Unhealthy" air quality includes AQI ranges where air pollution poses a significant risk to health for everyone, with more severe effects for sensitive groups:

AQI Range Category Health Impacts
201-300 Very Unhealthy Health alert: everyone may experience more serious health effects. Members of sensitive groups may experience significant health effects.
Health Tips for Very Unhealthy Air Quality

During very unhealthy air quality conditions, follow these tips to protect your health:

  • Stay indoors as much as possible and avoid all outdoor activities.
  • Use air purifiers with HEPA filters to reduce indoor pollution.
  • Keep windows closed to prevent outdoor air from entering.

Advice

  • Outdoor Activities: Stay indoors and avoid outdoor activities entirely. If you must go outside, use high-quality masks designed to filter out pollutants and limit exposure.
  • Indoor Air Quality: Invest in high-efficiency air purifiers with HEPA filters. Close all windows and doors to prevent outdoor air from entering.
  • Health Management: Avoid any exertion. Keep hydrated, follow prescribed medications, and seek medical advice if symptoms worsen. Use air conditioning or other indoor climate control methods to maintain a comfortable environment.
