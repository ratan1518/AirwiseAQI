Understanding Good Air Quality

What Does "Good" Air Quality Mean?

Air quality is considered "Good" when the AQI is between 0 and 50. This means that air quality is satisfactory, and air pollution poses little or no risk to the general population.

Here are the details:

AQI Range Category Health Impacts
0-50 Good Air quality is considered satisfactory, and air pollution poses little or no risk.
Health Tips for Good Air Quality

Even though air quality is good, maintaining a healthy lifestyle is always beneficial:

  • Continue regular exercise to maintain good health.
  • Stay hydrated and follow a balanced diet.
  • Keep your living spaces clean to avoid indoor pollutants.

Advice

  • Outdoor Activities: Enjoy outdoor activities freely. It's a great time for exercise, sports, and other physical activities.
  • Indoor Air Quality: Maintain regular cleaning routines. Ensure good ventilation but no special precautions are necessary.
  • General Wellness: Continue practicing a healthy lifestyle with a balanced diet, adequate hydration, and regular exercise.
