Airwise AQI - Poor Air Quality

Understanding Poor Air Quality

What Does "Poor" Air Quality Mean?

"Poor" air quality includes AQI ranges where the air pollution poses a health risk to everyone, and members of sensitive groups may experience more serious effects:

AQI Range Category Health Impacts
101-150 Poor Everyone may begin to experience adverse health effects; members of sensitive groups may experience more serious health effects.
Health Tips for Poor Air Quality

During poor air quality conditions, follow these tips to protect your health:

  • Avoid outdoor activities, especially prolonged or strenuous exercise.
  • Stay indoors and keep windows closed.
  • Use air purifiers to reduce indoor air pollution.

Advice

  • Outdoor Activities: Reduce time spent outdoors, especially for sensitive groups. Avoid strenuous physical activities and outdoor exercise.
  • Indoor Air Quality: Use air purifiers to reduce indoor air pollution. Keeping windows and doors closed can help minimize exposure.
  • Health Precautions: Monitor your health closely. If experiencing symptoms, seek medical advice. Keep a medication kit handy for those with pre-existing conditions.
